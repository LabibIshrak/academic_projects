Brown Corpus - Information
==========================

The Brown Corpus is used as the training dataset for all N-Gram models in this project.

Source: NLTK (Natural Language Toolkit) - Brown Corpus
Description: The Brown Corpus was the first million-word electronic corpus of English,
created in 1961 at Brown University. It contains 500 samples of English-language text,
totaling roughly 1 million words, compiled from works published in the United States in 1961.

Categories include:
- News
- Editorial
- Reviews
- Religion
- Hobbies
- Lore
- Fiction (Romance, Mystery, Science Fiction, Adventure, Humor)
- Government
- Learned (Academic)

Total words: ~1,014,312
Total sentences: ~57,340

Usage in this project:
- All words are lowercased during preprocessing
- Punctuation marks (. ! ?) are used as sentence boundaries
- The corpus is split into sentences for N-gram training
